## Problem

A tote carried by a Lifty that is held in place (Maintenance, Unassigned, AutoCharging on a charger or at very-low charge) keeps its unassigned subsets through reconciliation: `totesStackedInBlockedColumns` skips carried totes. `tryAssignCarriedSubsets` then takes the carrier. For an Unassigned carrier it writes `LiftySubsets` and `LiftyTotes`, and `AddPlanForLifty` rejects the plan. For a Maintenance carrier it sends the Lifty to a pick module.

## Change

- `blockedTotesAtEnd` (was `totesStackedInBlockedColumns`) also returns a carried tote whose carrier `isHeldInPlace`, so reconciliation drops its unassigned subsets. Allocation does not pick the tote again: `isGridToteBlocked` rejects a tote carried by a non-Delivery Lifty.
- A carrier that can move keeps its subsets. This PR does not reuse `isGridToteBlocked`, because `carried_subset_binding_test.go` keeps subsets on Inspecting and unbound Restocking carriers on purpose.
- `tryAssignCarriedSubsets` skips a carrier that `isHeldInPlace`.
- The drop warning names the reason: stacked in a blocked column, or carried by a held-in-place Lifty.
