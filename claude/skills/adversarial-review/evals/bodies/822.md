## Problem

`TestRunHBLoop_FiresSystemStopOnceOnZeroCrossing` failed at random with `"1" is not greater than or equal to "2"`: the second system stop never arrived.

Cause, in the test: `fireBatteryDepleted` drops a fire while `BatteryDepletedInFlight` is set, and the flag clears only when the delivery goroutine returns. The test drove the second zero-crossing without waiting for the first delivery. A late delivery goroutine made the loop drop the second crossing, so no wait could recover it. The production behaviour is by design: a pending stop still stops the agent.

## Change

Test only (`tmplt-agent/simulation/sim/heartbeat_ticker_test.go`):

- Wait for the first delivery, and for the flag to clear, right after tick 1.
- After the 5 ticks at 0, wait for the flag again and require exactly 1 stop, then do the second crossing.
- The fake action processor gets a `deliveryDelay` (20 ms in this test, ctx-aware, skipped at 0), so the slow-delivery path always runs.
- New helper `waitForDeliveryIdle`.
