## Problem

`precomputeHeuristics` calls `StraightLineDistances` once for each free cell, once per scheduling pass. The BFS set
the distance of a cell only when the cell left the queue, so other cells of the same level queued it again. The queue
grew past its capacity and the same cells were scanned again. Each scanned cell also hashed a 16-byte key into the
obstacle map. On the replay, this was 43% of the CPU time and 43% of the allocated bytes.

## Change

- `common/models/astar.go`: a cell gets its distance when it enters the queue, so it enters once. A scan passes a
  cell queued at the same level without stopping, and stops at a cell of an earlier level. The distances do not
  change.
- New `ObstacleGrid`: the obstacles in a flat grid-indexed `[]bool`. `StraightLineDistancesIn` takes one.
  `StraightLineDistances` keeps its signature and wraps it.
- `metascheduler/orders/order.go`: `precomputeHeuristics` builds the `ObstacleGrid` once per pass.
